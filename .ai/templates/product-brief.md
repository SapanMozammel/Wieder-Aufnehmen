# Product brief

## Problem and evidence

Target users, situation, existing alternatives, observed friction and assumptions.

## Outcome

Measurable user/business outcome and how to validate it.

## Scope and economics

Smallest complete journey, priorities, exclusions, delivery constraints,
operating cost, support burden and pricing assumptions where relevant.

## Acceptance and learning

Observable criteria, experiment or usability evidence, risks and what would
cause the team to change direction.
